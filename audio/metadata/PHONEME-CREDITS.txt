24 selected source recordings are losslessly wrapped in WAV.
16-bit signed PCM byte order is converted from PDF big-endian to WAV little-endian.
Numeric samples, sample rate, duration, pitch and gain are unchanged.
Consonants use the author's Cə, əCə demonstration.
/p t k/ use the source's aspirated allophones [pʰ tʰ kʰ], matching pen, ten, cat.
/g/ in the source is displayed as IPA /ɡ/ in the app.
See foundation-voice.json for symbol, source field/object, hashes and provenance.
The app is independent and is not endorsed by the source institution.

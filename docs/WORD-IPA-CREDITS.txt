Word IPA transcriptions — General American (broad phonemic notation)

Base dictionary: open-dict-data/ipa-dict, data/en_US.txt
https://github.com/open-dict-data/ipa-dict
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/open-dict-data/ipa-dict/master/data/en_US.txt

500 common words/phrases and 6 additional unique phoneme example words.
Dictionary variants are selected for the lesson's meaning; read is present-tense
/rid/, windy describes weather /ˈwɪndi/, tomato uses /təˈmeɪtoʊ/.
T-shirt's /ʃ/ is corrected; three missing phrases have curated transcriptions.
Broad notation uses r/l instead of narrow ɹ/ɫ, restores stressed STRUT /ʌ/
where the base dictionary uses ə, and omits stress on monosyllables.
The symbols i/u are broad American phonemes, consistent with the IPA lesson;
length marks and detailed allophones are not displayed. Existing recordings
are unchanged. These labels are dictionary pronunciations, not forced alignment
or a claim that every synthetic recording has been human-audited.

1017.16 adds 120 words in 10 daily-life categories. Dictionary candidates are
reviewed for stress and meaning rather than copied blindly. Overrides include:
umbrella /ʌmˈbrɛlə/, museum /mjuˈziəm/, octopus /ˈɑktəpəs/,
penguin /ˈpɛŋɡwɪn/, whale /weɪl/, stapler /ˈsteɪplɝ/.

from the homophone “cloze”, recorded as sourceText in the audio manifest.
